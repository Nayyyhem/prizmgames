Realm Stories

Welcome to Realm Stories, a narrative adventure game where you are the Chief of a medieval fantasy town.

Your town is full of colorful and quirky characters, each with their own stories to tell. As the Chief, you will see their stories play out on your screen, and make choices that will affect their lives and the future of your town.

UI Shapes

UI Shapes is a Unity asset that draws every UI effect procedurally from a single component: rounded corners, gradients, drop and inner shadows, glow, outline, bevel and background blur, mixed freely on any uGUI element.

Soft masking follows the real silhouette, buttons and toggles get a live-previewed tab per interaction state, and effects batch across the whole canvas, so busy screens stay at a handful of draw calls, even on mobile.
